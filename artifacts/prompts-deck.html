01 · Same job, bigger surface

Get the right information to the model at the right time.

That was always the job. Prompt engineering controlled one input; context engineering controls a system of inputs. It doesn't replace prompt engineering — it subsumes it.

02

The quiet death of
chain-of-thought.

For years, five words — "let's think step by step" — were magic.

02 · Wharton GAIL, 2026

"Think step by step" is fading.

  • +2–3%accuracy on reasoning models
  • +20–80%more time to run
  • risksometimes introduces errors
  • whythey already reason by default

The job moved: not teach the model to think, but decide when to spend reasoning.
